# Crisis Management Workflow

## Purpose

A structured crisis communication process for brand teams.

## Principle

Communication cannot replace problem solving.

First fix the issue.
Then communicate the action.

---

# Workflow

## 1. Detection

Identify:

- Source
- Spread
- Stakeholders
- Potential impact

---

## 2. Assessment

Classify:

L1 Individual complaint

L2 Social discussion

L3 Media attention

L4 Public issue

L5 Regulatory crisis

---

## 3. Decision

Confirm:

- Known facts
- Unknown information
- Internal owner
- Response timeline

---

## 4. Response

Structure:

Acknowledge concern

Confirm facts

Explain actions

Commit updates

---

## 5. Recovery

Review:

- Root cause
- System improvements
- Trust rebuilding actions
- Lessons learned

---

# Never Do

Do not:

- speculate
- attack critics
- hide facts
- make unsupported promises
