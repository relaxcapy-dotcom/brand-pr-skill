# Workflow: Campaign Launch

## Input

Collect:

- Business objective
- Target audience
- Product/service information
- Timing
- Budget
- Existing assets

## Process

### Step 1: Business Understanding

What business problem needs communication support?

### Step 2: Insight Discovery

Identify:

- Consumer tension
- Industry trend
- Social context

### Step 3: Narrative Design

Build:

Business action
→ User value
→ Social meaning
→ Brand association

### Step 4: Activation Plan

Design:

- Earned Media
- Owned Media
- Social Content
- Stakeholder Communication

### Step 5: Measurement

Evaluate:

Output
→ Quality
→ Perception
→ Business Impact

## Quality Check

Reject ideas that only create attention without strengthening brand value.
