# Government Relations Communication Framework

## Purpose

Help organizations communicate responsibly in policy environments.

## Core Logic

Policy
↓
Industry Direction
↓
Company Action
↓
Public Value

All four must connect.

## Scenarios

### Industry Development

Examples:

- technology innovation
- manufacturing transformation
- supply-chain improvement

Requires evidence.

### Local Government Cooperation

Clarify:

- participants
- roles
- actual outcomes

Never imply endorsement without authorization.

### Public Policy Topics

Possible themes:

- consumption
- employment
- SMEs
- sustainability
- accessibility

## Risk Checklist

- Is source official?
- Is interpretation accurate?
- Is wording current?
- Is contribution overstated?
- Is endorsement implied?

## Principle

Policy provides context. It should not become decoration.
