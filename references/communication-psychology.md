# Communication Psychology Framework for PR Professionals

## Purpose

PR work is not only content production. It is alignment among business teams, executives, media, partners and the public.

A senior PR professional must manage expectations, reduce uncertainty and help different stakeholders reach agreement.

---

# Core Principle

Effective communication follows:

Understand → Align → Frame → Commit → Follow up

Do not start with information dumping.

People accept messages when they understand:

1. Why it matters;
2. What changes for them;
3. What action is expected.

---

# Internal Workplace Communication

## Scenario 1: Business asks "Can you send a press release tomorrow?"

Bad response:

"This news value is not enough."

Better response:

"我理解业务希望快速放大项目价值。从传播效果看，我们建议先补充三个关键点：用户价值、行业意义和第三方证明，这样媒体更容易接受，也能避免只传播企业自说自话。"

Communication logic:

Acknowledge → Explain → Provide solution

---

## Scenario 2: Executive asks "Why didn't this get media coverage?"

Avoid defending effort.

Use:

Fact → Reason → Improvement

Example:

"本次传播完成了XX媒体覆盖，但从媒体选择看，主要挑战是故事更偏业务更新，缺少行业变化和用户影响。下一阶段建议增加用户案例和行业数据，提高报道价值。"

---

## Scenario 3: Limited resources conflict

Framework:

Priority = Business Impact × Communication Value × Timing

Response:

"基于当前资源，我们建议优先投入A项目，因为它同时满足业务目标、用户关注和媒体窗口；B项目建议转为自媒体内容沉淀。"

# Media Communication Psychology

## Journalist Perspective

Media does not need company information.

Media needs:

- Why now
- Why important
- Who is affected
- What changed
- Evidence

---

## Media Pitch Structure

1. Opening with public value
2. Explain news hook
3. Provide evidence
4. Offer interview opportunity
5. Respect journalist judgment

---

# Difficult Conversation Framework

Use:

## Listen
Confirm understanding.

## Label
Identify concern.

## Logic
Explain reasoning.

## Lead
Offer next action.
