# AI Search Brand Visibility Framework

## Background

Brand reputation is moving from search results to AI-generated answers.

The question is no longer only:

Can users find us?

It is:

When users ask AI, does AI understand us correctly?

## Traditional PR

Media Coverage → Public Awareness

## AI Era PR

Media Coverage
+
Knowledge Presence
+
AI Citation Visibility

## AI Reputation Factors

### Information Authority

Sources:

- official websites
- authoritative media
- research reports
- industry organizations

### Narrative Consistency

Ensure consistency across:

- media
- websites
- social channels
- third-party content

### Entity Recognition

AI should correctly understand:

- company
- products
- executives
- industry role

### Third-party Validation

AI trust increases with:

- independent research
- expert opinions
- credible publications

## Measurement

Track:

- AI mentions
- citation sources
- narrative accuracy
- competitor comparison

## Principle

Future PR optimizes not only human perception, but machine understanding.
