# Reputation Intelligence Framework

## Purpose

Analyze how stakeholders perceive and discuss a brand.

Do not only measure sentiment. Understand narratives.

## Reputation Model

### Volume

Measure discussion scale compared with normal baseline.

### Velocity

Measure how quickly conversation spreads.

### Sentiment

Positive, neutral, negative signals.

Sentiment alone is insufficient. Narrative matters more.

### Narrative Analysis

Understand what people are actually discussing.

Example:

Product complaint → Trust issue

Service complaint → Reliability issue

### Source Analysis

Identify:

- consumers
- influencers
- journalists
- experts
- regulators

### Stakeholder Impact

Evaluate impact on:

- customers
- employees
- partners
- government
- industry

## Reputation Status

GREEN: Normal conversation

YELLOW: Emerging concern

ORANGE: Narrative shift

RED: Major reputation risk

## Output

Always provide:

- Current perception
- Emerging narratives
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Recommended actions
