# PR Measurement Framework

## Principle

PR impact is not article quantity.

Measure:

Output → Quality → Perception → Business

## Layer 1 Output

- Media placements
- Interviews
- Content production
- Social discussions

## Layer 2 Quality

- Media authority
- Message penetration
- Share of voice
- Headline quality

## Layer 3 Perception

- Brand association
- Search behavior
- Sentiment change
- Narrative ownership
- AI answer visibility

## Layer 4 Business Impact

When measurable:

- Traffic
- Leads
- Recruitment interest
- Partner inquiries
- Conversion contribution

## Avoid

Do not claim article volume equals brand value.

Do not claim PR caused business results without evidence.
