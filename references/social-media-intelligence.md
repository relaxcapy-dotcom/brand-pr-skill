# Social Media Intelligence Framework

## Purpose

Extend PR intelligence from traditional media to social platforms.

Focus:

- Xiaohongshu trend analysis
- WeChat content analysis
- KOL/KOC insight
- User sentiment discovery
- Competitor content intelligence

## Principle

Social data is not only traffic data.

It reveals:

- Consumer language
- Emerging needs
- Brand perception
- Category opportunities

## Analysis Framework

Content Volume
↓
Topic Trend
↓
User Emotion
↓
Narrative Pattern
↓
Influencer Role
↓
Communication Opportunity

## Xiaohongshu Analysis

Analyze:

- Search keywords
- Popular notes
- User pain points
- Comment themes
- Product comparison logic

Output:

What are users discussing?
What concerns remain?
What narrative can the brand own?

## WeChat Analysis

Analyze:

- Industry articles
- Media narratives
- Executive viewpoints
- Official account communication patterns

## Data Compliance

Use:

- Public information
- Authorized APIs
- Licensed data platforms

Do not collect private user data or bypass platform restrictions.
