# Command: Campaign Brainstorm

## Purpose
Generate PR-worthy campaign ideas grounded in business reality rather than random creativity.

## Formula
Business Objective + Audience Tension + Social Emotion + Brand Asset + Real Action + Spread Mechanism

## Generate 3 Levels
1. Low-cost content play
2. Medium-scale integrated campaign
3. Flagship public event

For each idea provide: insight, big idea, business action, PR action, social mechanism, proof, risk, budget level and KPI.

Reject ideas that are only slogans, discounts, fake controversy or stunts unrelated to brand value.
