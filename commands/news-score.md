# Command: Newsworthiness Score

## Purpose

Evaluate whether a business event deserves proactive PR.

## Score Dimensions

- Industry significance: 15
- Consumer relevance: 15
- Originality: 15
- Evidence strength: 10
- Human story: 10
- Contrast: 10
- Timing: 10
- Public value: 10
- Policy relevance: 5

## Output

Provide:

- Total score
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Missing evidence
- Recommended improvements

## Rule

A company announcement is not automatically news.
