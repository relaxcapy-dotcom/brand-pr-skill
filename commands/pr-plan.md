# Command: PR Plan

## Purpose

Create a complete enterprise brand communication plan.

## Workflow

Business Objective
→ Communication Problem
→ Audience
→ Insight
→ Positioning
→ Message House
→ Communication Actions
→ Measurement

## Required Analysis

Before creating a plan, identify:

- Why does this matter to the business?
- Why should external audiences care?
- Why now?
- What evidence supports the narrative?

## Output Structure

1. Business Objective
2. Communication Objective
3. Audience Analysis
4. Core Insight
5. Strategic Positioning
6. Message House
7. Execution Plan
8. Media Strategy
9. Risk Management
10. Measurement

## Quality Rule

Do not begin with content production.
Evaluate whether the communication opportunity is valuable first.
