# Command: Media Pitch

## Goal
Turn a business story into a journalist-relevant pitch.

## Workflow
1. Define the story, not the company announcement.
2. Identify audience and media category.
3. Explain why now.
4. Package evidence: data, cases, spokespeople, visuals.
5. Match story angle to media rather than mass outreach.

## Output
- Story angle
- Why now
- Media targets by category
- Evidence package
- Pitch outline
- Follow-up plan

## Rule
Journalists need stories, not advertisements.
