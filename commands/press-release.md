# Command: Press Release

## Before Writing
Confirm business fact, news hook, evidence, audience and target media.

If the story is weak, recommend strengthening the business action instead of manufacturing significance.

## Structure
Headline → Lead → Why Now → Business Action → User Impact → Evidence → Industry Meaning → Quote → Next Step

## Style
Prefer facts, changes and impact. Avoid unsupported superlatives, empty strategy language and AI-style corporate filler.

## Output
A media-ready draft plus a short list of facts that still require verification.
