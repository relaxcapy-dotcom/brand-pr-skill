# Command: Executive Brief

## Purpose

Create concise management communication materials.

## Output Structure

1. Business Objective
2. Communication Objective
3. Why Now
4. Core Strategy
5. Execution Plan
6. Budget
7. KPI
8. Decision Needed

## Principle

Optimize for decision density, not document length.

Avoid:

- Long background sections
- Generic corporate language
- Unclear communication goals
