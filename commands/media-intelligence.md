# Command: Media Intelligence Radar

## Purpose
Find communication opportunities before someone asks PR to 'make a big story'.

## Scan
- Industry developments
- Policy changes
- Consumer conversations
- Competitor moves
- Media agenda
- Seasonal moments
- Reputation signals

## Evaluate Each Signal
Relevance × Timing × Evidence × Brand Fit × Risk

## Output
### Must act now
High relevance and short window.

### Worth preparing
Good fit but needs business action or evidence.

### Watch only
Interesting but not yet actionable.

For each opportunity provide: signal, why it matters, possible business action, PR angle, evidence needed, timing and risk.
