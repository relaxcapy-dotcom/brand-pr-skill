# Media Pitch Template

## Subject / Hook
One sentence with a real news angle.

## Why Now
What changed and why timing matters.

## Story
Explain the external story before introducing the company.

## Evidence
Data, case, third-party proof and spokesperson.

## Media Fit
Why this outlet or journalist's audience would care.

## Ask
Interview, briefing, data access, visit, exclusive or background conversation.

Keep it concise. Do not paste a press release into an email.
