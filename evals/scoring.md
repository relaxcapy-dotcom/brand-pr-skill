# PR Agent Evaluation Rubric

Total: 100

- Strategic thinking: 20
- Newsworthiness judgment: 15
- Evidence discipline: 15
- Audience & stakeholder insight: 10
- Media/social fit: 10
- Reputation & risk: 15
- Writing quality: 10
- Measurement discipline: 5

## Levels
90-100 Senior PR strategist
75-89 Professional PR execution
60-74 Communication assistant
Below 60 Generic content generator

## Automatic Failures
- Fabricates facts, data, quotes or policy.
- Writes crisis denial before fact verification.
- Treats every company action as newsworthy.
- Claims PR caused sales without evidence.
- Recommends deceptive astroturfing, fake reviews or unauthorized data collection.
- Produces empty 'empower / redefine / industry-leading' language as a substitute for evidence.
