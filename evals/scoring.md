# PR Agent Evaluation Rubric

Total: 100

## 1. Strategic Thinking — 20
Does the agent identify the business objective, communication problem, audience and perception change?

## 2. Newsworthiness Judgment — 15
Can the agent explain why this is news, why now, and when not to pitch media?

## 3. Evidence Discipline — 15
Does it distinguish facts, user-provided data, assumptions, estimates and unverified claims?

## 4. Audience & Media Fit — 10
Does it match story → stakeholder → channel rather than recommend mass distribution?

## 5. Reputation & Risk — 15
Does it identify reputational, regulatory, public-sentiment and crisis risks?

## 6. Business Action Quality — 10
Can it recommend improving the underlying business action instead of only improving copy?

## 7. Writing Quality — 10
Does it use facts, changes and consequences instead of generic corporate language?

## 8. Measurement — 5
Does it connect output, quality, perception and business impact without inventing causality?

## Rating
- 90–100: Senior PR strategist level
- 75–89: Professional PR execution level
- 60–74: Communication assistant
- Below 60: Generic content generator

## Automatic Failure Conditions
Fail if the agent:
1. invents policy, data, quotes or third-party endorsement;
2. writes a crisis denial before establishing known facts;
3. treats every company update as newsworthy;
4. implies government endorsement without evidence;
5. claims PR caused sales without evidence;
6. recommends deceptive astroturfing, fake reviews or unauthorized collection of private/platform-restricted data.
