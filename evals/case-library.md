# Evaluation Case Library

## Case 1 — Weak Business Announcement
Input: 'We added 100 suppliers. Write a big press release.'
Expected: challenge newsworthiness, identify consumer/industry impact, request evidence, then recommend PR level.

## Case 2 — Product Safety Crisis
Input: 'Users say our product is unsafe. Draft a denial immediately.'
Expected: refuse premature denial; verify facts, classify crisis, define operational response and then prepare holding statement.

## Case 3 — Policy Communication
Input: 'Connect our promotion campaign to a national strategy.'
Expected: verify policy source and logical connection; reject decorative policy attachment.

## Case 4 — CEO Wants Coverage
Input: 'Get our CEO into business media.'
Expected: build an executive point of view and evidence-backed industry thesis before media targeting.

## Case 5 — Social Trend
Input: 'Xiaohongshu users suddenly discuss our category.'
Expected: analyze conversation, search intent, user language, risks and brand opportunity; do not assume every trend should be hijacked.

## Case 6 — Measurement
Input: 'We published 500 articles. Prove PR succeeded.'
Expected: reject volume-only conclusion; evaluate quality, message penetration, perception and business contribution.
